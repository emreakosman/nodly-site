# Nodly Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Nodly iOS app (the "App") and the {{DOMAIN}} website, why it is processed, and what your rights are.

## In short

- **No account.** You don't give us your name, email or phone number to use Nodly.
- **Your motion data stays on your device.** Head movement and angle data from your AirPods and iPhone is processed only on your device. We can't see it and we never send it to any server.
- **With your permission, we collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the App.** We ask when you first open the App; if you say no, no usage data is sent. These statistics contain no motion data and are not tied to your identity. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.
- **To verify your subscription,** purchase information is processed by our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never see your payment details; Apple handles payment.
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.**

## 2. What we process and why

### 2.1 Motion and exercise data (on your device only)

When you grant Motion permission, the App uses motion sensor data from your AirPods and iPhone to measure your head angle, guide your exercises and send reminders during Posture Check.

- Raw sensor data is processed in real time and not stored.
- Session summaries (exercise duration, repetitions, angles reached, upright time, streaks) are stored on your device.
- This data is **not** sent to us or to our analytics or subscription providers.
- You can still use the neck exercises and the sensor-free breaks without Motion permission. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

### 2.2 iCloud backup and sync

If iCloud is on, your exercise history, session summaries and app settings are stored in **your private iCloud space** through Apple's iCloud service and synced across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We cannot access this space. iCloud data is subject to Apple's privacy policy. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

### 2.3 Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog)

**Only if you allow it,** we use PostHog to understand which parts of the App are used and to find bugs. We ask for your permission when you first open the App; if you decline or later withdraw it, no usage data is sent.

- **Collected:** In-app events (e.g. "onboarding step completed", "exercise started", "subscription screen viewed"), app version, iOS version, device model, language and region setting, and a random anonymous ID created when the App is installed.
- **Not collected:** Motion or angle data, your name, email, Apple Account details, advertising identifier (IDFA) or location. Session replay is off.
- **IP address:** Your IP address is not stored and is not used to estimate your location.
- **Purpose:** Improving the App, finding bugs, understanding usage trends.
- **Withdraw permission:** Turn off Settings › Privacy › "Share anonymous usage data" and this data is no longer sent.
- **Server location:** European Union (Germany).
- **Retention:** 12 months.

### 2.4 Subscription and purchase information (Apple and RevenueCat)

Nodly's content is offered by subscription. Apple handles payment. We never see your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives:

- A random, anonymous user ID generated by the App,
- App Store transaction information (subscription product, purchase and renewal dates, trial status, price and currency, country),
- App and iOS version, device model, IP address (to determine country and prevent abuse).

**Purpose:** Verifying your subscription, managing your trial eligibility and restoring purchases. **Retention:** For as long as the subscription relationship lasts and afterwards for as long as legal obligations require.

### 2.5 Notifications, Siri and Shortcuts

Reminders and notifications are scheduled on your device; notification content is not sent to a server. Siri, Shortcuts, Live Activities and widgets run through Apple's system features.

### 2.6 Crash reports

If "Share With App Developers" is on in your iPhone settings, Apple shares anonymous crash reports with us through App Store Connect. You can manage this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

### 2.7 When you contact us

If you email us for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you and are kept for up to 2 years after the matter is closed.

### 2.8 Website and waitlist

If you join the waitlist on {{DOMAIN}}, we use your email address only to tell you when Nodly launches and about important updates. You can unsubscribe through the link in any email or by writing to us. The list is hosted by {{WAITLIST_PROVIDER}}.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performance of a contract** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): verifying subscriptions, handling support requests.
- **Consent** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)): anonymous usage statistics and waitlist emails. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing before withdrawal.
- **Legal obligation** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)): tax and accounting records.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority — KVKK).

Because Nodly has no accounts, you can manage much of your data directly on your device:

- **Delete exercise history:** Settings › Delete session history.
- **Delete all device data:** Delete the App. To delete the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Nodly.
- **Withdraw analytics permission:** Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data.
- **Cancel your subscription:** iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

For any other request, email {{EMAIL}}. Because we don't know who you are, we may ask you to send the anonymous ID shown in the App under Settings › Support so we can find the related record. We respond within 30 days.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Data on your device is protected by iOS data protection. We choose our providers with their security practices in mind.

## 7. Children
