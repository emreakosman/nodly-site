Health & safety

Nodly is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Information in the app does not replace advice from a health professional.

If you have a neck, spine or musculoskeletal condition, a recent injury or surgery, dizziness, high blood pressure, or if you are pregnant, talk to a health professional before you start.

Move slowly and only within a comfortable range. Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness or any discomfort. If you feel lightheaded during a breathing exercise, return to normal breathing.

Angle measurements are estimates based on AirPods and iPhone sensors. Don't use Nodly while driving, and stay aware of your surroundings in Workout mode.
