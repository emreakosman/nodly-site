3. Health and safety

  • Talk to a health professional before you start, especially if you have a neck, spine or musculoskeletal condition, a recent injury or surgery, dizziness, high blood pressure, or if you are pregnant.

  • If you feel lightheaded during a breathing exercise, return to normal breathing.
